## 참고

- **eu-gmp-annex22-ai** (EudraLex Vol. 4, Annex 22): First EU GMP text written specifically for AI in medicinal product manufacture. Consultation closed 2025-10-07; still in development as of 2026-08 (EMA multistakeholder workshop 2026-06-30/07-01). Confirm the current version on the landing page before relying on it.
- **eu-gmp-annex11-draft** (EudraLex Vol. 4, Annex 11 (rev.)): Companion to Annex 22 — Annex 22 sits on top of Annex 11 controls (validation, audit trail, supplier oversight, cybersecurity).
- **eu-gmp-chapter4-draft** (EudraLex Vol. 4, Chapter 4 (rev.)): Published in the same 2025-07-07 consultation package as Annex 11 and Annex 22. No stable direct URL recorded — resolved from the EudraLex Volume 4 page.
- **eu-gmp-annex11-inforce** (EudraLex Vol. 4, Annex 11 (2011)): The legally applicable Annex 11 until the revision is adopted. Resolved from the EudraLex Volume 4 page because the historical direct URL has moved between ec.europa.eu and health.ec.europa.eu.
- **ema-reflection-paper-ai** (EMA/CHMP/CVMP/83833/2023): EMA's cross-lifecycle position, including the manufacturing phase. Read together with Annex 22.
- **ema-concept-paper-annex11** (EMA/INS/GMP/622941/2021): Explains the drivers behind the Annex 11 revision, including AI/ML and cloud.
- **ema-fda-good-ai-practice** (EMA/FDA joint guiding principles): Ten joint EMA/FDA principles issued January 2026. The FDA copy is catalogued separately as fda-good-ai-practice.
- **ema-ai-workplan** (EMA/HMA AI workplan to 2028): Regulatory roadmap rather than a binding requirement. Resolved from the EMA AI landing page.
- **eu-ai-act** (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689): Horizontal AI law that applies alongside GMP. For another language, swap /EN/ in the URL for the target locale (e.g. /DE/); EUR-Lex has no Korean version.
- **fda-ai-regulatory-decision-making** (Docket FDA-2024-D-4689): Risk-based credibility assessment framework for AI models, explicitly covering the manufacturing phase. Comment period closed 2025-04-07; still draft as of 2026-08 — check the landing page for a final version.
- **fda-ai-drug-manufacturing** (CDER FRAME discussion paper): The closest FDA counterpart to Annex 22 on the manufacturing side — not binding guidance.
- **fda-good-ai-practice** (FDA/EMA joint guiding principles): Same content as the EMA copy; kept separately so both official sources are archived.
- **fda-computer-software-assurance** (CSA final guidance): Risk-based validation approach for production/quality software; the FDA analogue to the Annex 11 validation expectations. An updated version was issued 2026-02-03 — the landing page always serves the current one.
- **fda-ai-device-lifecycle** (Docket FDA-2024-D-4488): Device-side total product lifecycle expectations. Relevant if the AI system is itself a regulated device or is reused across device and drug operations.
- **fda-pccp-ai-device** (PCCP final guidance): The reference model for managing planned model retraining/changes without a new submission — useful precedent for Annex 22 change control.
- **fda-data-integrity-cgmp** (Data Integrity Q&A): Baseline CGMP data integrity expectations that AI training and inference data must still satisfy.
- **fda-part11** (21 CFR Part 11 guidance): Records and signature requirements that apply to AI system outputs used in GMP decisions.
